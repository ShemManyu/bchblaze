LOCAL DEMO / EPHEMERAL TEST ASSETS

Three external test wallets.

These Node-hosted wallets sign actual BCH transactions over an encrypted local WizardConnect relay. The local ledger verifies BCH consensus scripts. Assets are synthetic, are not minted on Chipnet or Mainnet, and have no value. Restarting resets everything.

Open SwapCircle
  1. Open SwapCircle in three separate browser profiles, or use one browser and switch wallets between approvals. Private windows may share a session with each other.
  2. Click Connect wallet in the app, then Copy beside the pairing URI. Paste it below and choose Alice, Bob, or Carol. No wallet app or real keys are needed.
  3. Post Alice's NFT for Bob's category, Bob's for Carol's, and Carol's for Alice's. Every commitment is 01. The cards below contain their public category IDs.
  4. Build a matching circle, invite the other collectors, approve each exact trade, and settle. Test wallets automatically sign review requests, so use them only for this local demo.
